Ambigram Creator

Words that read the same upside down. Type, tune each letter pair, export.

Letter pairs

Each card is one glyph that has to read two ways. Edit the letters, or nudge either half until the shape works.

Tip: pairs like n/u, d/p, b/q, m/w, s/s, o/o are naturally symmetric. Awkward pairs usually get solved by stretching one half, or by letting two letters (rn) stand in for one (m).
